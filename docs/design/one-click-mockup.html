Nail Tracker: One-Click Mockups

Self-contained HTML mockups using the app's exact light and dark tokens from src/index.css at 375x740 phone dimensions. Covers (1a) the single-box Say screen with no scroll or gap, (1b) the one-tap Date row across full and compact headers, and (1c) the locked-height Home chart box across Today, This week, and This month in English and Vietnamese.

1a. Say Screen (375 x 740, Zero Scroll, Single Text Box)

One text box handles both dictated turns and pasted ChatGPT JSON replies. Removing the second textarea, the "Check the answer" button, and the mt-auto spacer lets the whole screen fit inside 375x740 before the keyboard opens.

1b. One-Tap Date Choice (Say, Adjustments, and Add Turn Header)

The chosen date is always visible and tappable to open the native date picker in one tap. Tapping Today or Yesterday updates that date immediately. No "Other day" button that reveals a second control.

1c. Home Screen: Today, This Week, and This Month (Locked 290px Chart Box)

Both the two-line comparison slot and the goal bar row stay reserved in all three periods, and the chart box stays locked at 290px high. Switching between Today, This week, and This month never shifts the Stats strip, the docked Add/Say bar, or the bottom navigation.

Why Today shows turn bars in order: Every technician logs turns, whereas work hours are optional and off by default, so turn bars in order always show how today's pay built up (up to 8 bars, then a +n bar).
